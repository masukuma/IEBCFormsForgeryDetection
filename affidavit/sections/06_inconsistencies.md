## INCONSISTENCIES IN THE AFFIDAVIT OF ARNOLD OCHIENG OGINGA

228. **THAT** the affidavit of Arnold Ochieng Oginga gives two different increases in the votes of William Ruto for five polling stations in Baringo County. One is in the list at paragraph 17 and the other is in the table at pages 8 to 13:

| Polling station | Increase in paragraph 17 | Increase in the table | William Ruto on the agent's form, in the table | William Ruto on the portal form, in the table |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kapedo Primary School 1 of 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 4 |
| Korelach Primary School 1 of 1 | 1 | 81 | 99 | 180 |
| Kamsino Primary School 1 of 1 | 81 | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Chepkirial Primary School 1 of 1 | 2 | 3 | 0 | 3 |
| Koipasiran Village 1 of 1 | 32 | 34 | 34 | 66 |

229. **THAT** the table also disagrees with itself. For Koipasiran Village its own figures of 34 and 66 give an increase of 32, not the 34 it states. For Chepkirial Primary School it records 62 votes for Raila Odinga on the agent's form and 61 on the portal form, but shows no variance for Raila Odinga. The agent's copy annexed at PDF page 94 reads 061 for Raila Odinga, as the original does.
230. **THAT** the Baringo totals in the table do not follow from its rows. Its rows give 3,420 votes for Raila Odinga on the portal forms, but the total it states is 3,421. Its totals for William Ruto, 268 on the agents' forms and 625 on the portal forms, differ by 357. The total variance it states is 359, which is the sum of its variance column. The difference of 2 comes from the row for Koipasiran Village.
231. **THAT** for Nairobi County paragraph 17 lists fifteen polling stations, while the table lists twelve. The table leaves out Kabete Vetlab Primary School polling stations 2, 3 and 4 of 12, for which paragraph 17 gives increases of 100, 83 and 106 votes. The Nairobi totals in the table are 3,470 votes for Raila Odinga, 1,843 votes for William Ruto on the portal forms and a variance of 1,217. Its twelve rows add up to 2,778, 1,487 and 928. The differences are 692, 356 and 289.
232. **THAT** the portal originals for the three stations left out record 233, 235 and 224 votes for Raila Odinga, which add up to 692. They record 123, 118 and 115 votes for William Ruto, which add up to 356. The increases that paragraph 17 gives for them add up to 289. The Nairobi totals in the table therefore include the three stations that its rows leave out. For Kabete Vetlab Primary School 12 of 12 and St Martin's School Kibarage 4 of 7 the table gives a variance but no figure for William Ruto on the agent's form.
233. **THAT** paragraph 21 of the affidavit states that the Form 34A for "Kadondo Grounds - Mishomoroni Polling Station 2 of 9" has not been uploaded in the portal. The Mombasa archive listed in paragraph 5 contains a Form 34A for Kadongo Grounds - Mishomoroni polling station 2 of 9. It is the file 1_34_A_001003000900302_Y3KK12CQ9IA02G2_20220809_203331.PDF, signed on 9th August 2022 at 20:33:31 East Africa Time, and its signature validates. The page at PDF page 46 of the affidavit, which has no caption, shows the same form. Its entries agree with those of that file, as I state at paragraph 208.
234. **THAT** paragraph 20 of the affidavit states that neither the agent's Form 34A for Maragi Primary School polling station 1 of 4 nor the portal form states any results. The portal original, the file 1_34_A_021106052404501_Y3KK10BBSGA009L_20220809_194914.PDF, was signed on 9th August 2022 at 19:49:14 East Africa Time. It has no figures in its column of votes for each candidate. Its polling station counts are filled in, and give 548 registered voters, 3 rejected ballot papers and 344 valid votes cast. The agent's copy annexed at PDF page 129 agrees with it, as I state at paragraph 223. The forms therefore bear out paragraph 20 as to the votes for each candidate.
235. **THAT** two pages are annexed for Kagera Primary School polling station 1 of 2 in Gatundu South Constituency, one captioned "IEBC PORTAL" and the other "IEBC (INITIAL) PORTAL". Paragraph 7 of the affidavit states that Forms 34A on the portal were replaced with forms "containing different results from what they had initially posted". The portal original, the file 1_34_A_022111055100301_Y3KK12CQDIA0045_20220809_200625.PDF, was signed on 9th August 2022 at 20:06:25 East Africa Time. The page captioned "IEBC PORTAL" agrees with it, as I state at paragraph 226. The page captioned "IEBC (INITIAL) PORTAL" differs from it in three figures, as I state at paragraph 227. It gives William Ruto 115 votes against 195 on the original, and both totals of valid votes as 176 against 256.
236. **THAT** in my opinion both pages annexed for Kagera Primary School were printed from the portal photograph. In the portal original the top edge of the sheet is curled over, against a dark background. Both annexed pages show the top edge curled in the same way against the same background, as annexure [XX]-41 shows. My measurements for the page captioned "IEBC (INITIAL) PORTAL" fall short of the levels I set for finding that it was made from the portal photograph, so this opinion rests on my examination of the images. If it is right, the figures on that page were changed on a copy of the signed portal image, and the page does not show an earlier version of the form.
237. **THAT** paragraph 7 also states that Forms 34A were deleted and uploaded as late as 19th August 2022. Each original I relied on carries a digital signature applied on 9th or 10th August 2022 which validates, so each of those files is as it was when it was signed. Examining images cannot show what the portal displayed at other times. I have not examined annexure "AOO1", which is a print-out of portal timestamps and not a Form 34A.
238. **THAT** statements resting on server logs, on access to the 1st Respondent's systems or on the accounts of witnesses, such as those in the affidavit of John Mark Githongo, cannot be tested by examining images of forms. I express no opinion on them.
